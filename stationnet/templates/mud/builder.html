WORLD SETTINGS / CHARACTER RESOURCE

Resource

Rename mana to suit this world, such as Energy, Stamina, or Power. Renaming changes only the wording. Turning it off hides the resource and makes spells unavailable in this world; characters keep their values. Procedural dungeons always use Mana. The setting is saved in world exports.

WORLD SETTINGS / OPTIONAL SYSTEMS

World life

Each system is off until you enable it, and is saved in world exports.

Clock

A clock gives the world hours of the day for /time, time conditions, and NPC schedules. A game day lasts the chosen number of real minutes; 1440 follows real UTC time, shifted by the starting hour.{% if game_time %} It is now {{ game_time.period }} ({{ '%02d:%02d' % (game_time.hour, game_time.minute) }}).{% endif %}

Relationships

NPCs remember how each player treats them. Regard runs from -100 to 100 and reads as hostile, unfriendly (below -20), neutral, friendly (20+) or trusted (60+). It changes through dialogue outcomes, triggers, gifts (/give), quests and attacks, and shifts prices by up to 10%. Hostile NPCs refuse to talk, and combat-capable ones attack. Players see it with /relations.

NPC LIBRARY / REUSABLE DEFINITIONS

NPCs

Optional behaviours: ambient speech, wander or patrol, and reactions to movement, speech, emotes, and items. See the custom world guide for JSON examples.

Daily schedules and relationships

With the world clock on, a schedule moves an NPC between rooms by the hour and sends it home otherwise. Entries run from from up to (not including) to and may wrap past midnight; the first matching entry wins. open: true lets a merchant or trainer trade there, and scheduled merchants and trainers are closed at other times. lines are shown in the room while the NPC is there. travel is walk (one room per tick, by the shortest route) or teleport. A schedule replaces wander and patrol.{% if not society.clock.enabled %} The clock is off, so schedules are paused.{% endif %}

{"schedule": {"interval_seconds": 30, "travel": "walk",
  "entries": [{"from": 8, "to": 18, "room": "ROOM-ID", "open": true,
               "lines": ["The smith hammers at a glowing blade."]},
              {"from": 18, "to": 22, "room": "ROOM-ID"}]}}

A relationship tunes how this NPC regards players: a starting default, gifts it values (item ID → change), greetings per tier, refuse_talk_below, its own quest_complete, attack and crime changes, and attacks_when_hostile.

{"relationship": {"default": 10, "gifts": {"ITEM-ID": 15},

WORLD SOCIETY / FACTIONS

Factions

Factions are groups such as guilds, towns, or gangs. Each character has a standing from -1000 to 1000 with each one, starting at its default. Standing sets a rank, and ranks decide how members behave: their price percent, whether they attack (attitude: hostile) or leave the player alone even when normally aggressive (attitude: peaceful), and whether they talk or trade. Choose an NPC's faction on its form. Standing changes through reputation outcomes and trigger actions, quest rewards, and defeating members. The reputation condition gates dialogue, quests, exits and stock. A relation passes a share of every change on to another faction; use a negative share for rivals. Players see public factions with /reputation and in their Standing panel.

WORLD SCRIPTING / TRIGGERS

Events

A trigger reacts when something happens: a player enters a room, uses or takes an object, says a phrase, helps defeat an NPC, picks a dialogue response, or completes a quest. If its conditions pass, its actions run in order. Use them for levers that open gates, bosses that unlock bridges, passwords, traps, and timed surprises. Actions can wait with "delay_seconds". Everything here travels in world exports as events.json.

Condition and action cheat sheet

Conditions (also usable on exits, quests, and dialogue): level, race, class, tag, attribute, skill, has_item, coins, quest, objective, plus {"type":"flag","scope":"player|world","key":"gate.open","value":"yes"} (or "min"/"max" for counters), {"type":"object_state","object":"…","state":"down"}, and {"type":"in_room","room":"…"}. Add "negate":true to reverse one, or group options with {"any":[…]}.

Actions: {{ trigger_action_types|join(', ') }}. Examples:

{"type":"message","to":"actor|room|other_room","text":"…"}
{"type":"set_flag","scope":"world","key":"wardens.slain","add":1}
{"type":"set_exit","exit":"EXIT ID","state":"open|locked|hidden|default","scope":"world|character"}
{"type":"set_object_state","object":"OBJECT ID","state":"down"}
{"type":"show","npc_instance":"NPC ID"}   {"type":"hide","object":"OBJECT ID"}
{"type":"spawn_npc","npc":"NPC TYPE ID","room":"ROOM ID","despawn_seconds":300,"delay_seconds":5}
{"type":"give_item","item":"ITEM TYPE ID","amount":1}   {"type":"experience","amount":50}
{"type":"heal","amount":10}   {"type":"damage","amount":5}   {"type":"teleport","room":"ROOM ID"}
{"type":"start_quest","quest":"QUEST ID"}   {"type":"complete_objective","objective":"OBJECTIVE ID"}
{"type":"run_trigger","trigger":"MANUAL TRIGGER ID"}
{"type":"learn_destination","destination":"DESTINATION ID"}   {"type":"forget_destination","destination":"DESTINATION ID"}
Condition: {"type":"knows_destination","destination":"DESTINATION ID"}   Dialogue outcome: {"type":"learn_destination","destination":"DESTINATION ID"}

Rewards go only to the player who caused the event. A shared trigger marked once rewards exactly one player. Damage never drops a player below 1 health. Chains stop after 4 nested triggers or 40 actions.
