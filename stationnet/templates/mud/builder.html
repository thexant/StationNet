WORLD SETTINGS / OPTIONAL SYSTEMS

World life

Each system is off until you enable it, and is saved in world exports.

Clock

A clock gives the world hours of the day for /time, time conditions, and NPC schedules. A game day lasts the chosen number of real minutes; 1440 follows real UTC time, shifted by the starting hour.{% if game_time %} It is now {{ game_time.period }} ({{ '%02d:%02d' % (game_time.hour, game_time.minute) }}).{% endif %}

Relationships

NPCs remember how each player treats them. Regard runs from -100 to 100 and reads as hostile, unfriendly (below -20), neutral, friendly (20+) or trusted (60+). It changes through dialogue outcomes, triggers, gifts (/give), quests and attacks, and shifts prices by up to 10%. Hostile NPCs refuse to talk, and combat-capable ones attack. Players see it with /relations.

Faction membership

With this on, players can belong to factions you mark as joinable on each faction's form below, using /faction in play or by choosing a starting faction at character creation. Off, factions keep working as world groups and any stored memberships lie dormant. If you require a starting faction, a character who qualifies for none still proceeds without one.

Crime

With crime on, taking a placed item whose state_json names an owner_faction or owner_npc is theft, /steal from <npc> picks pockets of NPCs with a steal behaviour, and attacking a peaceful NPC that belongs to a faction (or is lawful) is assault. Each NPC in the room may notice; Stealth, Agility and hiding lower the odds. A seen crime costs standing with the factions involved and regard with the witnesses, and the owner's faction fines the player. Guards (guard: true) attack players who owe their faction, until they /pay. Players can never be victims.

{% for kind in ('theft', 'pickpocket', 'assault') %} {% endfor %}

Housing

Housing does nothing until you add properties below. Switched off, nobody can acquire property and nobody is evicted for rent; owners keep their homes and storage, and can still pay or leave.

Vehicles and transit

Nothing happens until you add vehicles or lines below. Switched off, nobody can board, refuel, or buy a ride; riders can still drive, get off, and arrive.

WORLD SOCIETY / FACTIONS

Factions

Factions are groups such as guilds, towns, or gangs. Each character has a standing from -1000 to 1000 with each one, starting at its default. Standing sets a rank, and ranks decide how members behave: their price percent, whether they attack (attitude: hostile) or leave the player alone even when normally aggressive (attitude: peaceful), and whether they talk or trade. Choose an NPC's faction on its form. Standing changes through reputation outcomes and trigger actions, quest rewards, and defeating members. The reputation condition gates dialogue, quests, exits and stock. A relation passes a share of every change on to another faction; use a negative share for rivals. Standing does not make a player a member; turn on Faction membership in the world-life settings to let players join. Players see public factions with /reputation and in their Standing panel.

WORLD SCRIPTING / TRIGGERS

Events

A trigger reacts when something happens: a player enters a room, uses or takes an object, says a phrase, helps defeat an NPC, picks a dialogue response, or completes a quest. If its conditions pass, its actions run in order. Use them for levers that open gates, bosses that unlock bridges, passwords, traps, and timed surprises. Actions can wait with "delay_seconds". Everything here travels in world exports as events.json.

Condition and action cheat sheet

Conditions (also usable on exits, quests, and dialogue): level, race, class, tag, attribute, skill, has_item, coins, quest, objective, plus {"type":"flag","scope":"player|world","key":"gate.open","value":"yes"} (or "min"/"max" for counters), {"type":"object_state","object":"…","state":"down"}, and {"type":"in_room","room":"…"}. Add "negate":true to reverse one, or group options with {"any":[…]}.

PLAYER HOMES

Housing and storage

A property is a set of rooms only its owners may enter. A unique property has one holding at a time; an instanced property gives every holder a private copy of its template rooms, sharing the template's text and exits. A primary owner can invite co-owners, who must accept, share the rent, and use owners-only storage. Unpaid rent past the grace period ends a co-owner's share, or the whole holding when the primary owner lapses. Items are never lost: whenever a holding ends, stored items return to whoever stored them. Players use /house and /storage; add storage to a room in its room panel.

GETTING AROUND

Vehicles and transit

Players /board a vehicle and then /go and /travel move it. A vehicle without an interior carries its rider alone; one with an interior carries everyone aboard inside a private copy of that room while the operator steers. Exits can need a mode (water, rail, air - your own words) or be on foot only, and wilderness presets can need a mode too. Public vehicles stand where you place them; owned ones come from a grant_vehicle outcome, so a dialogue can sell one. Lines carry riders between stops for a fare after a set time.
