Import or upgrade a world

World ZIPs carry everything a world needs: rooms, content, quests, merchants, events, dungeons, progression tuning, and their own races, classes, skills, abilities, and effects. Importing adds a world without changing any other world. Choosing a file checks it completely and lists any problems before you import. A file whose ID matches an installed world is offered as an upgrade that keeps that world's characters. World files live in data/mud_worlds/.

Upgrading replaces the world's rooms and content, and any World Forge drafts of it become stale until rebased.
