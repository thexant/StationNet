    Import and export fidelity

    XLSX retains formulas, common number formats, cell colors, text styling, borders, alignment, sheet names, and frozen panes. XLS and ODS import preserve formulas and values, but some styles and layout may change. ODS export preserves sheets, formulas, values, and number formats; colors, borders, alignment, and freeze panes may be lost. CSV and TSV use the active sheet’s calculated values only and lose formulas, other sheets, styles, and charts. PDF uses the browser print view of the active sheet. Charts and macros are not imported or embedded in exported files; charts remain in saved StationNet workbooks.
